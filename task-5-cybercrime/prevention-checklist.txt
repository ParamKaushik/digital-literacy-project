Stay Safe Online Checklist:

- Never share your OTP or PIN with anyone
- Do not click on unknown or suspicious links
- Always verify the sender before making payments
- Use strong and unique passwords
- Enable two-factor authentication
- Avoid using public Wi-Fi for financial transactions
- Check app permissions before installing
- Regularly update your apps and devices
- For UPI: Always check payment requests carefully before approving
- For UPI: Never accept unknown payment collect requests
